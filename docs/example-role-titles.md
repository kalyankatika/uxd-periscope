# Example organization: role and team titles

Updated September 12, 2026. These are proposed titles for Periscope’s fictional UXD example, informed by public design roles. They are not a reproduction of another company’s organization or a claim that one title structure is universally best.

Use recognizable Product Designer, Design Researcher, Content Designer and Design Engineer roles, with specializations where they clarify responsibility. Keep people-management roles distinct from Staff and Principal individual contributors. AI design spans research, conversation, interaction patterns and working prototypes. Existing IDs, reporting lines, disciplines, capacity and projects remain unchanged.

## Reference points

- [OpenAI: Product Designer, Codex](https://openai.com/careers/product-designer-codex-san-francisco/) — product design alongside research and engineering for an AI software engineer.
- [OpenAI: Product Designer, Design Systems](https://openai.com/careers/product-designer-design-systems-san-francisco/) — design systems remains an explicit product-design specialization.
- [Anthropic careers](https://www.anthropic.com/careers/jobs) — public roles include Design Engineer, AI Capability Development; supports a design-engineering track.
- [Figma careers](https://www.figma.com/careers/) — Product Designer, Design, Dev, & AI Tools combines product craft and AI tooling.
- [Figma: Product Designer, AI Models](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/figma/jobs/5711913004) — describes connecting AI research and design principles.
- [Airbnb: Staff Service Designer](https://careers.airbnb.com/positions/7514027/) — distinguishes Staff individual contributors from design managers/directors.

The exact seven-team grouping and leadership titles below are a Periscope design choice, not titles attributed to those companies.
